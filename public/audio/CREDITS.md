# Recorded sound credits

The game serves edited mono Opus files from this directory. The source recordings carry the [CC0 1.0 public domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| Game sound | Recording and artist | Edit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plate glass | [Glass.ogg by egomassive](https://freesound.org/people/egomassive/sounds/536763/), based on Craig Smith’s CC0 recording “G26-07 Windows Breaking” | Trimmed the onset, retained the falling shards, and normalized the level. |
| Helicopter | [Helicopter Rotor Loop.flac by qubodup](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/187681/) | Mixed to mono, selected a stable blade cadence, and crossfaded the loop. |
| Falling and settling masonry | [Concrete Breaks Several Denoised by loganzsound](https://freesound.org/people/loganzsound/sounds/843339/) | Selected two separate impacts, removed the quiet lead-ins, and trimmed their tails. |
| Fatal shark bite | [Screaming “Help” by Fabrizio84](https://freesound.org/people/Fabrizio84/sounds/457961/) (CC0 1.0). | Mixed the call and intake of breath with the credited civilian scream recording below, then filtered the cry into submerged bubbles. |
| Civilian panic calls | [Male_Scream_6.wav by tonsil5](https://freesound.org/people/tonsil5/sounds/416540/) and [Woman Screaming by DanJFilms](https://freesound.org/people/DanJFilms/sounds/845164/), both CC0 1.0. | Cut three short alarm reactions, filtered low rumble, and faded the starts and tails. |
