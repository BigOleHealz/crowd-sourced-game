    Reload a handheld weapon
    Press R or tap Reload. You can reload early. Switching weapons cancels the reload and keeps the rounds left in each weapon. When R is assigned to replay a completed supply run, use J to reload.
    Scuba spearfishing
    Choose Coral Reef on the Brickell district map. Look for the red buoy with a red-and-white dive flag off Pier C at East Quay. At the surface press G or tap Equip scuba. Hold C or Dive to descend. Aim the crosshair at a fish and press F, click, or tap Spear. Hold Space or Rise to surface and refill your air.
    Harbor Key Beach Club
    Follow the beach club waypoint in Brickell free roam. Press E or tap the prompt at Marisol’s counter to order, on the deck to dance, or by a lounge chair to sit. Move to get up.
    Harbor Key Basketball
    In Brickell free roam, follow the Harbor Key Basketball waypoint. Press E or tap Join basketball. On your turn face the hoop, hold F, click and hold, or hold Shoot. Release when the meter reaches gold. Press E or walk off the court to leave.
    East Quay Marina · 10 m platform
    Follow the dock north to the ladder. Press Space or tap Climb ladder to reach the platform. Use the ladder again to descend. At the tip, press Space for a straight dive or C for a forward 1½ in tuck. On touch, choose the matching button. Steer in the air and press Space or tap Open for entry in the gold zone. Seven judges mark execution. The middle three scores count, multiplied by the announced difficulty.
    Palma de Sal
    Choose Palma de Sal on the Brickell map and follow the centaur court to valet. Enter Fan Hall and follow the guest lift signs. Press E or tap the elevator prompt to choose any suite floor or the penthouse pool. Concierge can assign you a suite if you want one. The Salt Arcade across the street has live blackjack at its northwest table.
    Building interiors
    Enter a street gallery and look for the illuminated ELEVATOR panel. Press E or tap Use elevator, then choose a floor. Use E at a room door to open or close it. Return to the panel and select Ground lobby to leave.
    Biscayne Mutual Bank
    In Brickell free roam, find the freestanding limestone bank one block south of Mangle Café, on the south side of the cross street west of Brickell Avenue. Enter under the green BANK sign and talk to the teller. Choose Rob bank to start the alarm. When the teller sets the $150 cash parcel on the counter, press E or tap Collect to pick it up. Police pursuit follows your personal Police setting. You can opt in at the counter.
    Harbor Key Market
    In Brickell free roam, walk over dropped cash or supplies to collect them. Press E at a staffed checkout to shop. An unattended counter can be looted while its owner is down. Open your bag with I or tap the wallet to use an item. Cash and goods drop when you are defeated and reset when you leave the session.
    Club Lucero
    Follow the Club Lucero waypoint to the cabaret. Walk to the west booth and press E or tap Sit for a private dance. Press E, Esc or Leave dance at any time.
    Hotel regulars
    At Hotel Maravela in Brickell, walk up to Marisol or Della and press E to talk. Choose a topic with the mouse or keys 1–3. Press Esc or walk away to leave.
    Optional police
    In free roam, turn Police on to enable a wanted level for yourself. Stealing civilian vehicles, defeating people and destroying vehicles draws patrols. Break sight or outrun the patrol to lose stars. You can turn Police off at any time.
    Marina Sprint
    Choose Brickell · Marina Sprint in the map menu, then stop a road vehicle under the Mangle Café start banner and press T. Drive through the marked gates to the marina. Press T near the finish for another run.

    Crouch / prone
    On foot, press C to crouch or Z to lie prone. Move with WASD or the arrows. Press the same stance key again to stand, or the other key to switch.
    Jump / climb
    Space. Face a low ledge and press Space to grab it and pull up.
    Look around
    Move the mouse to turn. Click the world to capture the mouse. Press Esc to release it for the HUD.
    Choose a seat
    Press E near a vehicle to board. Once aboard, press 1 for driver or pilot, or 2 for gunner. Occupied seats stay with their crew. Press E to exit. On foot, press 1–8 to select equipment or Q to cycle. Press 0 to put away or draw the last selection. On touch, tap Put away or Draw.
    Civilian traffic
    Look for cars downtown, on either causeway, around Harbor Key and Lantern Point, or on the Outpost depot, ford and ridge tracks. Step into the lane to stop a civilian car. Walk up and press E to take the wheel. W/S accelerates or brakes, A/D steers, H sounds the horn, and E parks and exits.
    Patrol boats
    Look for B on the Brickell map. Press E alongside a patrol boat from shore or water. W/S sets throttle, A/D steers. A second rider uses the rear turret. Aboard, 1 selects helm, 2 selects gunner, and E disembarks.
    Solana superyacht
    Find Y at the Lantern Point marina. Walk up the gangway or swim to the stern ladder. Press E at the forward helm, use W/S for throttle and A/D to steer, and press E to stand. Passengers can walk and use their weapons on deck. Press E at the bow gun to crew it, then hold F or click to fire. Land the helicopter on the aft H.
    Gunner seat
    Press E by a crewed vehicle. Look in any direction, then hold F or click to fire the turret. Press E to leave.

    Brickell drawbridges
    Approach the center of either channel in Solana to raise the bridge. In the pickup or GT, line up with the road and keep your speed up to jump the gap.
    Cheetah
    Look for the Cheetah outside Casa de Luma on Lantern Point. E enters or exits. W accelerates, S brakes or reverses, and A/D steers.
    Vehicle fuel ⛽
    Stop at the pumps, land at an air service point, or bring your boat alongside a fuel buoy. Press U or tap Refuel to fill your tank. Fuel locations are marked on the map.
    Repair garage ⚒
    Park a damaged road vehicle in the yellow bay for three seconds. Find Bayfront Motor Works at the south end of Bayfront Avenue, the yacht club bay at Lantern Point, or the rural Depot Motor Pool.
    Drive rover
    W / S to drive or reverse, A / D to steer. E parks and exits.
    Tracked tank
    Find T beside the Brickell helipad. E boards or exits, W/S drives, A/D turns the tracks, and the mouse aims the turret. F or left click fires. A second player can use E beside the occupied tank to take the exposed roof gun. Aim with the mouse and fire with F or click. On touch, drag to aim and hold Fire turret. Use 1 or 2 to take an empty seat.
    Strike jet
    Look for J near the starting area. E boards or exits. Move the mouse up to take off or climb, down to descend, and left or right to bank and turn. Keep the cursor above or below center to complete a loop. Center it to hold your current pitch, even straight up or inverted. W/S adjusts throttle. Hold F or click for the cannon and press R for a forward missile. Tap B in flight to drop a bomb. Slow down before landing.
    Fly
    Movement keys steer the helicopter. Hold F or click to fire the pilot's fixed forward machine guns; press R for a forward missile. A passenger controls the separate belly turret with the mouse and fires it with F or click.
    Helicopter cargo hook
    Hover over a road vehicle or patrol boat and hold K to lower the hook. Keep K held until the cargo attaches, then fly. Press L to release the load. Releasing K before attachment reels the hook in.

    Table rules

    Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Aces count as 1 or 11. The dealer stands on all 17s. A two-card blackjack pays 3 to 2, other wins pay even money, and ties return your wager. Each hand uses a fresh shuffled deck. Use your in-game wallet or play for free. Leaving an unfinished hand or sitting idle for two minutes forfeits the wager.
