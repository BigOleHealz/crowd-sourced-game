    Field controls

    Marina Sprint
    In Brickell free roam, stop a road vehicle under the Mangle Café start banner and press T. Drive through the marked gates to the marina. Press T near the finish for another run.
    Forward / back
    W / S or ↑ / ↓
    Strafe left / right
    A / D or ← / →
    Swim
    Enter deep water, then use the movement keys to swim. Walk out on a sloping bank, or face a low quay or dock and press Space to pull yourself up.
    Sprint
    Hold Shift
    Crouch / prone
    On foot, press C to crouch or Z to lie prone. Move with WASD or the arrows. Press the same stance key again to stand, or the other key to switch.
    Jump / climb
    Space. Face a low ledge and press Space to grab it and pull up.
    Look around
    Move the mouse to turn. Click the world to capture the mouse. Press Esc to release it for the HUD.
    Choose a seat
    Press E near a vehicle to board. Once aboard, press 1 for driver or pilot, or 2 for gunner. Occupied seats stay with their crew. Press E to exit. On foot, 1–4 select weapons.
    Civilian traffic
    Look for cars downtown, on either causeway, around Harbor Key and Lantern Point, or on the Outpost depot, ford and ridge tracks. Step into the lane to stop a civilian car. Walk up and press E to take the wheel. W/S accelerates or brakes, A/D steers, and E parks and exits.
    Patrol boats
    Look for B on the Brickell map. Press E alongside a patrol boat from shore or water. W/S sets throttle, A/D steers. A second rider uses the rear turret. Aboard, 1 selects helm, 2 selects gunner, and E disembarks.
    Solana superyacht
    Find Y at the Lantern Point marina. Walk up the gangway or swim to the stern ladder. Press E at the forward helm, use W/S for throttle and A/D to steer, and press E to stand. Passengers can walk and use their weapons on deck. Press E at the bow gun to crew it, then hold F or click to fire. Land the helicopter on the aft H.
    Gunner seat
    Press E by a crewed vehicle. Look in any direction, then hold F or click to fire the turret. Press E to leave.
    Vehicle missiles

    Brickell drawbridges
    Approach the center of either channel in Solana to raise the bridge. In the pickup or GT, line up with the road and keep your speed up to jump the gap.
    Vesper GT
    Look for GT outside Casa de Luma on Lantern Point. E enters or exits. W accelerates, S brakes or reverses, and A/D steers.
    Repair garage ⚒
    Park a damaged road vehicle in the yellow bay for three seconds. Find Bayfront Motor Works at the south end of Bayfront Avenue, the yacht club bay at Lantern Point, or the rural Depot Motor Pool.
    Drive rover
    W / S to drive or reverse, A / D to steer. E parks and exits.
    Tracked tank
    Find T beside the Brickell helipad. E boards or exits, W/S drives, A/D turns the tracks, and the mouse aims the turret. F or left click fires.
    Strike jet
    Look for J near the starting area. E boards or exits. Move the mouse up to take off or climb, down to descend, and left or right to bank and turn. Keep the cursor above or below center to complete a loop. Center it to hold your current pitch, even straight up or inverted. W/S adjusts throttle. Hold F or click for the cannon and press R for a forward missile. Tap B in flight to drop a bomb. Slow down before landing.
