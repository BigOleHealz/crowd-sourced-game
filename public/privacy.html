Accounts are optional. You can play as a guest without creating one.

What is stored

If you create an account, the game stores your username, a salted password hash if you use a password, or your Google account's unique ID if you use Google sign in. It also stores a hashed session token, lifetime kills, deaths, time played, and connection sessions. The game does not store your Google email address or profile name.

How it is used

Your username appears on the in-game scoreboard. Your account panel shows your saved totals. The session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days; it is HttpOnly and sent only to the game site.

Storage limits

This preview currently uses a free Render database that expires after 30 days. Accounts and stats can become unavailable and may be deleted after that unless the database is upgraded. The free database has no automatic backups.
